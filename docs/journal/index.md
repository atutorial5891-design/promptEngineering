# Learning Journal

A dated log of experiments, insights, and open questions. Each entry is its own file, named `YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md`, starting from the [entry template](entry-template.md).

**Why keep a journal?** Prompt engineering is empirical. What you *measured* is worth more than what you *read*. Entries feed the [Progress dashboard](../learning-path/progress.md) and, over time, your own pattern catalog.
