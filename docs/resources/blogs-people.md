## Staying current (monthly routine)

1. Skim provider changelogs and new prompting guides, and log the changes in [What's new](../reference/whats-new.md).
2. Read one new paper or engineering post, and add it to the relevant module's references.
3. Re-run one older lab on the newest model, and note what changed.
