# DeepLearning.AI Short Courses

Short (about 1-2 hour) courses with hosted notebooks. Most are free. Catalog: [deeplearning.ai/courses](https://www.deeplearning.ai/courses/).

| Course | Instructors / partner | Covers modules | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [ChatGPT Prompt Engineering for Developers](https://www.deeplearning.ai/courses/chatgpt-prompt-eng) | Isa Fulford (OpenAI) and Andrew Ng | 03, 04, 24 | The classic starter course. Two core principles, plus summarize, infer, transform, and expand. Updated in late 2025 |
| AI Prompting for Everyone | Andrew Ng | 02, 04 | Non-coding prompting habits for everyday use |
| [DSPy: Build and Optimize Agentic Apps](https://www.deeplearning.ai/courses/dspy-build-optimize-agentic-apps) | Databricks / DSPy | 19 | Signatures, modules, MLflow tracing, and optimizers. The best intro for module 19 |
| Evaluating AI Agents | Arize | 15, 18 | Component and end-to-end evals, LLM-as-judge, production monitoring |
| Improving Accuracy of LLM Applications | Lamini / Meta | 18, 22 | The eval, prompt, and fine-tune loop |
| Large Multimodal Model Prompting with Gemini | Google | 13 | Image and video prompting patterns |
| [AI Agents for Image and Video Generation](https://www.deeplearning.ai/courses/ai-agents-for-image-and-video-generation) | Google | 13, 25 | Image and video prompting plus evaluation loops. Updated in 2026 |
| [AI Agents in LangGraph](https://www.deeplearning.ai/courses/ai-agents-in-langgraph) | LangChain and Tavily | 15 | Build an agent from scratch, then rebuild it with LangGraph |
| Agentic AI | Andrew Ng | 15, 17 | Reflection, tool use, planning, and multi-agent design patterns |

Courses without a direct link can be found by searching the catalog for their title.
