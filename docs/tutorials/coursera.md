# Coursera

Structured specializations from universities and companies. You can audit most courses for free; certificates are paid.

| Course | Provider | Covers modules | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Prompt Engineering for ChatGPT](https://www.coursera.org/learn/prompt-engineering) | Vanderbilt (Dr. Jules White) | 02, 03, 04, 06 | A *pattern language* for prompts (persona, flipped interaction, template, and more). Great for building intuition |
| [Prompt Engineering Specialization](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/prompt-engineering) | Vanderbilt (Dr. Jules White) | 02-08 | The three-course series that extends the course above |
| [Google Prompting Essentials](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/prompting-essentials-google/) | Google | 02, 04, 13 | A five-step prompting framework, multimodal prompting, and a reusable prompt library. Beginner level, under 10 hours |

!!! note
    These courses are aimed at *users* of chat assistants more than *builders* of LLM systems. Take them for intuition and patterns, then take the DeepLearning.AI and Udemy courses for engineering depth.
